# Creeper World 4

## What does randomization do to this game?

The Farsite Expedition campaign (20 missions) is opened up: mission access,
unit unlocks (cannon, mortar, terp, and the rest), and ERNs are shuffled into
the multiworld item pool. Completing mission objectives and missions sends
checks. Missions are open: any mission whose unlock you hold is playable,
in any order your units can handle.

## What is the goal?

Beat the final mission, Ever After, by completing its four objectives.

## What items can appear in other players' worlds?

Mission unlocks, unit unlocks (including units the campaign never grants,
like the Airship and Bertha), progressive ERNs, and build-limit increases.

## How do I install the game mod?

See the setup guide: install BepInEx into Creeper World 4, add the
CW4Archipelago plugin, and connect from the in-game Archipelago panel.
